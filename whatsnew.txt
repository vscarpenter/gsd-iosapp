iOS (recommended)

▎ GSD 3.7 adds due times and puts buttons on your reminders. Set a time of day on a due date, and its reminder follows that time. All-day tasks remind you at 9 a.m. Each reminder now has Complete and Snooze 1 hour buttons, so you can act on it without opening the app.
▎
▎ GSD can also flag a task that isn't marked urgent as its due date gets close. You choose how close in Settings, or turn it off. Nothing moves until you choose Mark urgent.
▎
▎ The task editor now asks before discarding your changes, and only when you made some. On iPad, ⌘? opens Help. Several fixes also protect the tasks on your device during sync and when you delete your account.
▎
▎ As always, GSD works fully offline. Syncing is optional and only happens when you sign in.
