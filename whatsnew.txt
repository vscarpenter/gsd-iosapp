iOS (recommended)

▎ GSD 3.6.3 fixes the matrix on iPad. A task card now keeps its own size instead of stretching to match a busier quadrant beside it. All four quadrants also stay in view, and a long list scrolls inside its own quadrant.
▎
▎ GSD 3.6 added Saved, a place to set a task aside with a short note or a link and return it to the matrix when you're ready. GSD 3.6.1 made Saved sync across your devices.
▎
▎ As always, GSD works fully offline. Syncing is optional and only happens when you sign in.
