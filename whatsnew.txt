iOS (recommended)

▎ GSD 3.0 is built for iOS 27. The system chrome takes the new Liquid Glass; your tasks stay on the same warm paper.
▎
▎ • A layered app icon. The four quadrant tiles and the check now render in every Home Screen style, including Clear and Tinted.
▎ • Swipe on iPad. Matrix cards use the system swipe actions: swipe right to complete, left to snooze or delete, the same gestures as iPhone.
▎ • Room to work. The tab bar tucks away while you scroll and comes back when you scroll up, so the capture field is the only chrome left in full.
▎ • Resize without losing your place. Open the editor, change the window size (iPhone Mirroring, iPad multitasking), and the editor stays open.
▎ • Today's Focus in any style. The widget marks overdue and due-today tasks by shape, so it reads in Tinted and Clear modes too.
▎
▎ As always, GSD works fully offline. Syncing is optional and only happens when you sign in.

macOS (recommended)

▎ GSD 3.0 is built for macOS 27. The window, sidebar, and toolbar take the new Liquid Glass; your tasks stay on the same warm paper.
▎
▎ • A layered app icon that renders in every icon style.
▎ • The sidebar runs edge to edge and its selected row reads clearly in every state.
▎ • Resize the window across the compact boundary and an open editor stays open.
▎ • Toolbar items yield in a sensible order when a window gets narrow: search stays, Edit and Show Completed move to the overflow menu first.
