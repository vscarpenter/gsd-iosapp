iOS (recommended)

▎ GSD 3.6.1 fixes Saved sync. Items you set aside on one device now reach every other device, including the ones saved before this update. They appear after the first sync on this version.
▎
▎ GSD 3.6 added Saved: set a task aside without losing it, keep a short note or a link beside the title, and return it to the matrix when you're ready. It also brought the layered app icon, iPhone Duo support, the large Today's Focus widget, and sharing from Safari.
▎
▎ As always, GSD works fully offline. Syncing is optional and only happens when you sign in.
